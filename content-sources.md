## Current content

All six listings were generated with AI assistance as fictional examples for this project: Kijani Digital Studio, Threadway Skills House, Rise Kitchen Workshop, Buildpath Training Yard, Greenstep Learning Garden, and Brightbench Skills Lab.

For every listing, the name, course, fee, duration, and description are fictional. District and town/city names are illustrative place labels, not verified provider addresses. No provider research, live availability checks, certification verification, or real contact information is included.
